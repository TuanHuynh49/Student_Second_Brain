LEARNING OBJECTIVES

Load course materials, represent them consistently, and prepare them for retrieval.

CORE CONCEPTS

A document loader extracts text from sources such as PDF, TXT, and Markdown files. PDF extraction quality depends on the file. Scanned pages may require OCR, while ordinary text-based PDFs can often be parsed directly.

A Document represents a source document. A DocumentChunk represents a smaller section derived from a document. A chunk should retain its relationship to the original document so retrieved information can be traced to a source.

A suggested document schema includes doc_id, title, content, subject, year, and owner. A chunk can contain chunk_id, doc_id, text, and metadata.

Chunking divides long text into smaller passages. Chunks that are too small may lose context; chunks that are too large may contain unrelated information. Overlap can preserve context between neighboring chunks.

Metadata may include title, source, subject, date or version, owner, page, and section. Metadata supports filtering and citations.

APPLICATION TO STUDENT SECOND BRAIN

The ingestion pipeline loads lesson plans, extracts text, creates chunks, and preserves metadata. Retrieval results should include enough source information to support citations.

KEY TAKEAWAY

Do not discard source identity or metadata during loading and chunking. The system needs them to retrieve, filter, and cite knowledge reliably.
